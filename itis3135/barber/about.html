Head Barber & Owner

MEET THE BARBER

University No Money Barber was founded on one idea: every student deserves a clean, professional haircut — regardless of budget. As someone who grew up around barbershop culture, the owner has been cutting hair for over 5 years, honing a craft that blends classic technique with modern style.

Based near UNC Charlotte, the shop quickly became a favorite for students who wanted quality work at prices that make sense on a college budget. Word of mouth and a growing client base proved the concept works.

Whether it's your first visit or your fiftieth, every client gets the same attention to detail and the same level of care. That's the promise.
